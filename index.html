Research

Three connected areas of inquiry

My work examines not only whether treatments work, but whether people can access, receive, and sustain effective care in real-world systems.

01

Addiction & Behavioral Health

Treatment access and retention, medications for opioid use disorder, emerging therapies, gambling-related services, and behavioral mechanisms that influence engagement in care.

02

Maternal & Perinatal Health

Substance use and mental health during pregnancy and postpartum, perinatal toxicology practices, treatment continuity, and inequities in how health systems identify and respond to risk.

03

Health Systems & Policy

Medicaid policy, treatment capacity, implementation, access disparities, and the organizational and policy conditions that determine whether evidence-based care reaches patients.

About

Research focused on what happens between evidence and access.

I am a health services and policy researcher whose work sits at the intersection of addiction, maternal health, behavioral health systems, and public policy. My research uses population-level data, quasi-experimental methods, mixed methods, and implementation-oriented approaches to understand where care systems succeed, where they fail, and how access can be improved.
